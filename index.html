Independent by design

A personal approach to practical automation.

Hammersley is an independent consultancy. When you get in touch, you work directly with me — from the first conversation through to designing, building and refining a solution.

I enjoy understanding how things work, spotting where a process can be improved and making technology easier to use. My background in education and department leadership has given me years of experience explaining complex ideas clearly, solving practical problems and understanding the needs of the people doing the work.

I believe AI should be useful, understandable and introduced for a reason. It should make running a business easier, not give you another complicated system to manage.
